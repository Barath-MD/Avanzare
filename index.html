ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

Where Early 2K's Legacy Meets Modern Ingenuity

Avanzare is the premier techno-Cultural festival of the IEEE Student Branch of Kongu Engineering College — a four-day convergence of innovation, competition, and enchantment held at the legendary campus of Kongu Engineering College.

Since its founding in 2003, the festival has grown into one of the most prestigious collegiate competitions, drawing the sharpest minds of 500+ Students across Tamil Nadu.
